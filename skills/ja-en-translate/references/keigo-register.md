# Keigo and Register Mapping

Keigo is a relationship system, not a vocabulary layer. Translate the relationship, not the words.

## Levels used by this skill

| Key | Japanese | Typical use |
|---|---|---|
| `none` | Plain form (だ / 普通体) | Friends, statutes, academic body text, narration |
| `teinei` | 丁寧語 (です・ます) | Default polite: notices, docs, acquaintances |
| `sonkei-kenjo` | 尊敬語 + 謙譲語 on top of です・ます | External business, professors, customers, formal requests |

## JA→EN: what keigo becomes

| Japanese feature | English realization |
|---|---|
| です・ます vs. plain | Usually no difference; at most contractions vs. no contractions |
| 尊敬語 (いらっしゃる, おっしゃる, ご覧になる) | Neutral verbs (come, say, see) + polite framing in the sentence ("Would you…", "Thank you for…") |
| 謙譲語 (伺う, 拝見する, 申し上げる) | Neutral verbs; humility shows in framing ("I'd be grateful if…"), never "humbly" |
| させていただく | Usually "I will…" / "We are pleased to…"; drop the permission nuance unless real permission is meant |
| Customer address (お客様) | "you" / "customers" |
| Status switching within a text (e.g., a character slips into タメ口) | Mark it in English through word choice or rhythm; it often signals emotion or intimacy |

## EN→JA: choosing the level

Ask: who writes to whom, in what setting?

| Relationship | Level |
|---|---|
| Student → professor / university office | `sonkei-kenjo` |
| Company → customers / public | `teinei` (notices) or `sonkei-kenjo` (direct customer letters) |
| Employee → external party | `sonkei-kenjo` |
| Colleagues, classmates (not close) | `teinei` |
| Close friends, family | `none` |
| Narrative fiction | Each character's own level |

English cues that raise the level: "Dear…", "I would be grateful", "kindly", "at your earliest convenience". Cues that lower: first names, "Hey", contractions, emoji.

## Common keigo errors (check in critique)

| Error | Wrong | Right |
|---|---|---|
| 二重敬語 | おっしゃられる / ご覧になられる | おっしゃる / ご覧になる |
| 謙譲語 used for the other person | 先生が申された | 先生がおっしゃった |
| 尊敬語 used for oneself | 私がいらっしゃいます | 私が参ります / 伺います |
| さ入れ言葉 | 読まさせていただく | 読ませていただく |
| させていただく overuse | 説明させていただかせていただきます | ご説明いたします |
| お/ご mismatch | ご連絡させていただく (fine) vs. お連絡 ✗ | ご連絡 |
| バイト敬語 | 〜のほうでよろしかったでしょうか | 〜でよろしいでしょうか |
| Internal person exalted to outsiders | 弊社の田中部長がおっしゃいました | 弊社の田中が申しておりました |
