# Built-in Glossary (JA⇄EN)

Lowest priority: any user glossary overrides these. Entries with a context apply only in that context. Extend through EXTEND.md rather than editing this file, so updates to the skill don't overwrite your terms.

## Names

- **Japanese people in English**: family name first by default. Under an inter-ministerial agreement of 25 October 2019, government documents have written romanized Japanese names surname-first since 1 January 2020, with the surname in capitals where the two parts must be told apart (YAMADA Haruo). Switch with `name_order_ja_people: given-first` (e.g., when a journal or the person themself uses Western order).
- **Western people in Japanese**: given name first, katakana, ・ separator: ベネディクト・アンダーソン.
- **Chinese and Korean names**: family first in both languages. Use the person's own romanization if known.
