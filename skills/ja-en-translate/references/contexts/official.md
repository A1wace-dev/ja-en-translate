# Context: official

Government documents, laws and regulations, residence/immigration procedures, municipal notices, forms, certificates, university regulations, terms of service, contracts.

## Defaults

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Japanese style | だ・である for statutes and regulations; です・ます for notices to the public |
| Keigo | `teinei` for notices; none for statutes |
| English | Formal, precise; "shall" only where the source creates an obligation |
| Notes | `footnote`, used for ambiguity and for terms with no official English |
| Restructuring | Minimal. Keep clause numbering and structure |

## Core stance

**Use established official renderings, and never resolve ambiguity silently.** In legal and administrative text, a "clearer" sentence can change rights and obligations.

## Sources of official English

- Laws: Japanese Law Translation database (日本法令外国語訳データベース, japaneselawtranslation.go.jp). Its translations are for reference only (the Japanese text alone has legal effect), and its Standard Legal Terms Dictionary governs terms like 在留資格 → status of residence, 在留カード → residence card.
- Ministries and agencies publish English names; use them (出入国在留管理庁 → Immigration Services Agency of Japan; デジタル庁 → Digital Agency).
- Municipal multilingual pages often have their own English; follow them when translating that municipality's documents.

If an official rendering exists, use it even if another phrasing reads better. If none exists, translate literally and add a footnote.

## Rules

- Keep article/paragraph/item structure. Japanese Law Translation (JLT) translations render 条/項/号 as Article/paragraph/item and 前項/前条/同項 as "the preceding paragraph", "the preceding Article", "the same paragraph". Numbering style differs between translations (e.g. "Article 31-3, paragraph 1, item 2" vs. "paragraph (2), item (i)"): when the law has a JLT translation, copy its style; otherwise pick one style and keep it.
- Obligation and permission are exact: しなければならない → shall; することができる → may; することができない → may not; してはならない → shall not; 努めなければならない → shall endeavor to (check the JLT dictionary for the law at hand).
- Defined terms: keep the same rendering every time; capitalize in English if the source defines them.
- Dates: keep 和暦 with Western year in parentheses when converting: 令和8年 → 2026 (Reiwa 8). Keep the source's era usage in the translation if the document is a form.
- Amounts, deadlines, addresses: copy exactly; never round.
- Forms: translate labels; leave fields; keep the original label in parentheses when users must fill the Japanese form (氏名 Name / 氏名).

## Disclaimer

For anything with legal effect (contracts, applications, immigration documents), add to the summary — not the translation body: "This translation is for reference. The Japanese original is authoritative; confirm with the issuing office for procedures."

## Critique focus

- Every obligation/permission word mapped exactly?
- Official names and terms used where they exist?
- Any ambiguity silently resolved? (It must be flagged instead.)
- Numbers, dates, clause numbers identical?
