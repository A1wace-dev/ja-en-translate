# Same source, different contexts

The context profile changes far more than tone. These examples were written for this repository to show what each profile does; they are not output logs.

## JA → EN

**Source**

> 申し訳ございませんが、提出期限を一週間ほど延ばしていただくことは可能でしょうか。体調を崩してしまい、資料の分析が終わっておりません。

| Context | Translation | What the profile did |
|---|---|---|
| `business` (student → professor) | Would it be possible to extend the deadline by about a week? I have been unwell and have not finished analyzing the materials. I'm sorry for the inconvenience. | Keigo becomes polite framing ("Would it be possible…"), not "humbly". The apology moves to the end, where English email puts it. |
| `casual` (to a classmate) | Any chance we could push the deadline back a week? I got sick and haven't finished going through the data yet, sorry! | Same request at a peer's register. 申し訳ございません shrinks to "sorry". |
| `subtitle` (42 chars/line) | Could I get an extra week?<br>I got sick and I'm behind. | Compressed to the speech act. Details that the scene shows anyway are dropped. |

**Source**

> 本稿は、地域の日本語教室が外国人住民にとって「居場所」として機能してきた可能性を示唆するものである。

| Context | Translation | What the profile did |
|---|---|---|
| `academic` | This paper suggests that community Japanese classes may have served foreign residents as an *ibasho*, a place where one feels one belongs. | 示唆する stays "suggests" and 可能性 stays "may": the claim keeps its strength. 居場所 is kept with a gloss on first use. |
| `marketing` (NPO flyer) | For many foreign residents, our Japanese class is more than a class. It's a place to belong. | Transcreation: the hedge is gone because a flyer makes a claim on the group's own behalf. The profile allows this; the academic one never would. |

## EN → JA

**Source**

> Thanks for the update! Could you send me the final version by Friday? Let me know if you need anything from me.

| Context | Translation | What the profile did |
|---|---|---|
| `business` (to an external partner) | ご連絡いただきありがとうございます。恐れ入りますが、最終版を金曜日までにお送りいただけますでしょうか。こちらで準備が必要なものがございましたら、お知らせください。 | Full frame with 尊敬語・謙譲語. The request is softened with 恐れ入りますが. "you" and "me" disappear. |
| `business` (internal, to a colleague) | 共有ありがとうございます。最終版を金曜日までに送ってもらえますか？ こちらで必要なものがあれば言ってください。 | 丁寧語 only. Same structure, lighter keigo. |
| `casual` (to a friend) | 連絡ありがと！最終版、金曜までに送ってもらえる？ なんか手伝えることあったら言ってね | Plain form, no pronouns, natural chat punctuation. |

**Source**

> The system can process up to 500 requests per second.

| Context | Translation | What the profile did |
|---|---|---|
| `technical` | このシステムは毎秒最大500件のリクエストを処理できます。 | 〜することができる → 〜できる. Number and unit exact. |
| `marketing` | 毎秒最大500リクエストを処理。大量のアクセスにも対応します。 | 体言止め headline plus a benefit line that restates the spec. The figure and "最大" (up to) are kept: marketing may reshape wording, never facts. |
